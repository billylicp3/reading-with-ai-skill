# Reading companion design specification

## Reading workflow

The guide supports browsing before opening the PDF, returning for explanations while reading, and optional reflection afterwards. It does not prescribe a single path. Use **Reading with AI** as the tool name. Place paper identity first, then a collapsed **How to use Reading with AI**, then three continuous stages:

1. **Before reading: Meet the paper and its reading focus**
2. **During reading: Navigate sections and clarify concepts**
3. **After reading: Check understanding and explore further**

Use clear stage anchors rather than extra competing main tabs. Keep factual, explanatory and navigational content visible as appropriate; hide only supporting detail.

## Before reading

State text type, purpose, a useful entry question and the limits of the evidence. Explain 1–3 essential abbreviations before their first substantive use, if genuinely needed. Build an SVG map that conveys argument relationships, not boxes containing paragraphs. Label it as a reader-created scaffold unless reproducing an authorised original figure. Do not invent causation, sequence or consensus. Include source locations, an SVG title/description and equivalent readable text. On narrow screens reflow into a vertical equivalent rather than shrinking labels. Both versions must retain the same relationships, page references and conclusion.

## During reading

Use a light three-column table: **Section and source location | Author's argument (paraphrase) | Guiding question**. Show the main argument directly. Put explanatory notes, invented examples and important qualifications in keyboard/touch-accessible native details elements within the middle cell. Questions invite attention; do not require form entry. On narrow screens turn each row into labelled vertical blocks without losing the three-way correspondence.

Provide a keyword index as a subsection, with short definitions and matching section names/numbers. Do not make it a fourth main stage. Source references must distinguish the supplied PDF order from printed/journal page numbering. By default show source locations as text, leaving source reading to the reader's PDF application; no embedded PDF viewer or paper-download link in the guide. A separate workshop access hub may link to a lawful source.

## After reading

Use a small number of relevant scenarios, clearly labelled if invented. Display each case directly; a **Show a possible interpretation** disclosure reveals one interpretation, not a reader diagnosis or a reported study. Open questions may remain open; do not imply they have one definitive answer.

For fixed understanding checks, use a short prompt, radio choices and **Submit answer**. Missing choices prompt selection. Correct and incorrect responses use text plus colour and reveal a prepared explanation, with source locations when applicable. Changing a choice clears old feedback. Fixed questions check selected distinctions, not overall proficiency. Do not claim a page is evaluating open reflections with live AI.

Follow-up prompts are visible and independently copyable. Each contains enough paper identity (authors, year, full title and DOI when available) to make sense in a fresh chat. Ask subsequent AI to use the supplied/accessed text, acknowledge missing access, distinguish explanations from source claims, and label invented examples. Copy buttons must offer manual selection when clipboard access is unavailable.

## Visual style and interactions

Prefer warm cream (#f5f3ee or similar), white content surfaces, dark readable text and deep green (#195c56 or similar) accents. Use restrained green/amber notes, fine borders, readable body text around 17–18px and generous line spacing; serif headings and system sans-serif body are a useful default. No decorative dashboard metrics, thick card walls or full-application chrome. Adjust density for actual content rather than adding empty space or shrinking text.

Provide A− / reset / A+ controls with a visible percentage. Resizing must affect paragraph, table and diagram-equivalent text. Keep focus rings, semantic headings, labels and adequate contrast; do not rely only on hover or colour. Native details/summary and buttons should work by keyboard. Test the whole SVG, not only page overflow. Print must expose necessary explanations and remove controls; do not print the answers as though the reader submitted them.

Generated files must work offline with inline styles and script. No external libraries/fonts, network calls, analytics, cookies, localStorage, reader input persistence, login, embedded chatbot or automatic upload. No notebook, progress checkboxes or submission workflow. A copyable prompt is not sent automatically.

## Language

An English edition has English UI, content and dynamic feedback. A Chinese-support edition uses Chinese UI, explanations and feedback while retaining useful English terminology, paper titles and bibliographic details; do not duplicate every sentence bilingually. When two editions are requested, preserve facts, page mapping and exercise purpose without requiring literal translation. User-requested language changes override these defaults.

## Source and academic-use note

Briefly disclose AI assistance and actual text accessed; distinguish verification by AI from human review. Do not claim a full original translation. Keep a concise statement that use depends on learning goals and instructor/task rules, and that AI assistance should be documented as applicable. Readers remain responsible for checking what they adopt. Do not fabricate personal learning, discussions or quotations. A guide is not an approval for assessed work.

Public HKU references (checked 9 November 2026; revisit before making new institutional claims):
- [TALIC: Academic integrity and core principles](https://talic.hku.hk/innovation/ai-ethics/core-principles/)
- [HKU Libraries: Acknowledging and citing GenAI use](https://libguides.lib.hku.hk/AI-literacy/how-to-cite-genai-usage)

These sources support responsible-use principles, not this three-stage layout or any mandatory HTML reading procedure. Summarise them briefly in the generated guide. Source copyright, permitted uploads and redistribution remain separate questions. Do not infer redistribution permission from a free download.

## Acceptance

Check actual access, paper type, claims versus explanations, source page mapping, map semantics and accessible mobile equivalent. Inspect at desktop and phone widths, enlarged text and print. Test disclosures; unanswered/correct/incorrect/reselected radio states; copy success or clear manual fallback; resize limits. Report unavailable checks truthfully. Template validation does not establish that a newly generated paper analysis is accurate.
